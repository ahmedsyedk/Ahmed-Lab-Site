MS & PhD Research Opportunities – Medicinal Chemistry & Organic Synthesis

Dr. Syed K. Ahmed is accepting motivated and enthusiastic MS and PhD students to pursue research in Medicinal Chemistry, Organic Synthesis, and Drug Discovery at the USC Alfred E. Mann School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences.

Students interested in innovative research involving small-molecule synthesis, medicinal chemistry, and drug discovery are encouraged to apply.

Research Areas

  • Medicinal Chemistry & Drug Discovery
  • Organic Synthesis & Synthetic Methodology
  • Small-Molecule Design & Development

How to Apply

Interested students should email their CV and a brief statement of research interests.
